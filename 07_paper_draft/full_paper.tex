% ---------------- Abstract ----------------
\begin{abstract}
\noindent
Public administration in Vietnam is undergoing rapid national digital transformation under
governmental directives such as Project 06. However, ordinary citizens encounter substantial
barriers when navigating grassroots-level administrative procedures (commune and ward levels).
These difficulties arise from complex statutory language, multi-tiered regulatory decrees, and
colloquial citizen queries characterized by shorthand, unaccented typing, multi-intent requests,
and personal demographic conditions. General-purpose Large Language Models (LLMs) frequently
exhibit hallucinations regarding administrative fees and statutory processing deadlines, succumb
to prompt injection attacks, and lose context across extended multi-turn dialogues.

This paper presents \sys{}, an enterprise-grade, domain-adaptable legal artificial intelligence
assistant that unifies a deterministic, zero-hallucination \textbf{Strict Engine (System 3)} with a
conversational, multi-domain \textbf{Friendly Engine (System 4)}. Operating entirely on local,
privacy-preserving infrastructure, \sys{} deploys an optimized SLM runtime (Ollama
\texttt{qwen3:4b-instruct}), hybrid sparse-dense retrieval (SQLite FTS5 BM25 combined with Qdrant
\texttt{bge-m3} embeddings and a \texttt{bge-reranker-v2-m3} cross-encoder), and structured
table-reasoning tools. Multi-turn conversational degradation is resolved via: (1) a deterministic
dialogue state machine equipped with \emph{Clarify State Isolation}; (2) a colloquial demographic
condition extractor tailored to Vietnamese phrasing; (3) grounded document question-answering over
citizen-uploaded files (Excel, Word, PDF); and (4) real-time, fact-checked web search grounding via
DuckDuckGo.

We report comprehensive empirical evaluations across controlled developmental benchmarks and unseen
blind test suites, reporting Wilson 95\% confidence intervals. On the official blind evaluation suite
(\textbf{Blind Golden Set}, $n=553$ cases), the \textbf{Strict Engine} attains \textbf{78\% behavioral
accuracy} (431/553, 95\% CI: 74\%--81\%), \textbf{73\% Top-1 retrieval accuracy} (295/406, 95\% CI:
68\%--77\%), \textbf{80\% Top-3 retrieval} (323/406, 95\% CI: 75\%--83\%), a Mean Reciprocal Rank
(\textbf{MRR}) of \textbf{0.759}, and \textbf{nDCG@5} of \textbf{0.752}. On the official
\textbf{HOLDOUT-4} benchmark ($n=106$ turns, 77 scorable queries), the system achieves \textbf{85\%
behavioral accuracy} (90/106, 95\% CI: 77\%--90\%), \textbf{78\% Top-1} (60/77), \textbf{87\% Top-3}
(67/77), \textbf{MRR of 0.824}, and \textbf{nDCG@5 of 0.805}. On the \textbf{Friendly Engine (System 4)},
the system achieves \textbf{91\% general answer accuracy} (29/32), \textbf{100\% accuracy and citation
fidelity on citizen-uploaded documents (17/17)}, \textbf{100\% accuracy on tabular calculation and
counting}, and \textbf{zero numerical hallucinations across live web searches (0/16)}. Operating in
deterministic rule mode, \sys{} delivers a median CPU retrieval latency of only \textbf{57--85 ms}
(p95 of 85--122 ms) on consumer hardware, while generative SLM composition executes in 3.19 s (p50)
on an edge GPU (GTX 1660 SUPER 6GB). Safety benchmarks demonstrate \textbf{100\% PII redaction recall}
(12/12) and \textbf{100\% prompt injection defense} (11/11). Furthermore, an empirical study of
LLM-as-a-judge evaluators demonstrates that 7B SLM judges identify only 51\% of planted factual
fabrications (\kpa{}\,$=0.02$), validating our architectural decision to anchor administrative
factuality in deterministic database grounding rather than generative self-evaluation.

\vspace{4pt}
\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} \sys{}, Legal AI Assistant, Vietnamese Public Administration, Multi-Turn
Context Management, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), Local SLMs, Hybrid Retrieval, Grounded
Evaluation, Wilson Confidence Interval.
\end{abstract}

% ======================= 1. INTRODUCTION =======================
\section{Introduction}
Grassroots public administration at the commune, ward, and township levels constitutes the primary
frontline interface between citizens and state governance in Vietnam. It encompasses essential civil
procedures such as vital registration (birth, marriage, death registration, guardianship), household
business registration, social welfare assistance, social insurance, and residency declarations. While
the National Public Service Portal (\texttt{dichvucong.gov.vn}) provides digitized access to statutory
procedures, ordinary citizens continue to experience significant hurdles:

\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item \textbf{Discrepancy Between Statutory Language and Colloquial Inquiries:} Official announcements
use strictly codified legal vocabulary, whereas citizens express inquiries in informal dialects,
teencode shorthand, unaccented text, or vernacular idioms.
\item \textbf{Multi-Turn Dialogue Dependency and Demographic Specifics:} Inquiries rarely manifest as
self-contained prompts. Citizens typically initiate conversation with broad queries (\emph{``I want to
get married''}), followed by fragmentary quantitative inquiries (\emph{``How long does it take and how
much does it cost?''}), and subsequently introduce personal qualifiers (\emph{``What additional
documents are required if one partner was previously divorced?''}).
\item \textbf{Stringent Zero-Tolerance for Hallucination in Public Governance:} In administrative law,
inaccuracies carry direct legal and financial repercussions. An AI assistant hallucinating fee
exemptions, fabricating expediting deadlines, or directing citizens to improper jurisdictional bodies
severely disrupts civic compliance and damages institutional trust.
\item \textbf{On-Premise Infrastructure Constraints and Data Sovereignty:} Local government agencies
operate within air-gapped or compute-constrained server environments, precluding reliance on
high-latency, costly commercial cloud APIs that risk leaking citizen Personally Identifiable
Information (PII).
\end{enumerate}

To address these compounding challenges, this work introduces \sys{}, an enterprise-scale, dual-engine
architecture that integrates the deterministic verification core of \textbf{System 3 (Strict Mode)}
with the flexible conversational and document-processing capabilities of \textbf{System 4 (Friendly
Mode)}. The primary scientific and engineering contributions include:

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Dual-Engine Architecture:} Decoupling the pipeline into a \emph{Strict Mode} (statutory
grounded citations, deterministic SQLite FTS5 BM25 indexes, verifiable safety gates) and a
\emph{Friendly Mode} (local SLMs, Qdrant dense vector search with cross-encoder reranking, citizen file
QA, external web grounding).
\item \textbf{Robust Multi-Turn Context State Engine:} A state-tracking mechanism with \emph{Clarify
State Isolation} that eliminates ``phantom procedure'' memory pollution during disambiguation turns,
alongside dynamic field inheritance on dialogue return paths.
\item \textbf{High-Efficiency On-Premise Serving:} Operating entirely on consumer edge hardware
(NVIDIA GTX 1660 SUPER 6GB) via Ollama (\texttt{qwen3:4b-instruct}), delivering median CPU retrieval
latencies of \textbf{57--85 ms} and sub-4s response generation for complex conversational synthesis.
\item \textbf{Transparent, Statistically Rigorous Empirical Evaluation:} Reporting complete benchmark
evaluations across 1,352 standardized golden cases with Wilson 95\% confidence intervals on System 3,
alongside comprehensive multi-scenario benchmarks on System 4 (document QA, tabular calculation, web
search), and an empirical audit of the limitations of LLM-as-a-judge frameworks.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Strict Engine (System 3) Operational Principles}
The \textbf{Strict Engine} operates on a zero-hallucination mandate. Procedures are anchored by their
official national procedure identifiers (\texttt{proc\_id}), ministerial decision numbers, and
standardized field chunks (dossier components, fees, statutory processing times, submission addresses,
and legal decrees).
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Rule-Based Routing:} Queries addressing specific procedural requirements are routed
directly to database extractors, bypassing stochastic generation entirely.
\item \textbf{Safe Refusal:} When a citizen asks about a procedure outside commune-level jurisdiction,
or requests fees where official data is unpublished, the engine explicitly outputs a safe refusal
rather than interpolating missing figures.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Multi-Turn Context State Engine: Clarify State Isolation}
A critical failure mode in conversational RAG is \emph{phantom procedure contamination}, where candidate
procedures presented during a disambiguation turn inadvertently pollute dialogue memory. \sys{}
implements Clarify State Isolation:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item When ambiguous queries (e.g., \emph{``Tôi muốn làm thủ tục hộ tịch''}) trigger clarification cards,
direct routing tasks are cleared.
\item The dialogue state machine \textbf{does not record any procedure into} \texttt{active\_proc\_id}
or the visited procedures list.
\item If the citizen follows up with a generic question (\emph{``Mất bao lâu?''}), the state tracker
recognizes that ambiguity remains unresolved and re-prompts the user to select one of the suggested
procedures, completely preventing the model from hallucinating deadlines for an arbitrarily chosen
candidate.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Friendly Engine (System 4): Citizen Document QA \& Live Web Search}
To serve broader civic inquiries beyond the fixed national gazetteer, System 4 introduces a flexible
multi-domain workspace:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Tabular Data Calculation (\texttt{tabletool}):} Citizen-uploaded Excel files are indexed as
relational tables. Queries requiring counting (\emph{``lớp có bao nhiêu bạn nam''}) or filtering are
executed programmatically rather than via LLM text extrapolation, lifting tabular computation accuracy
from 0\% to 100\%.
\item \textbf{Live Web Grounding:} Users can trigger DuckDuckGo-powered web search. The assistant ingests
snippets, attaches citation indices [n], and refuses to invent numerical values if web results yield no
definitive answer.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Future Enterprise Scaling Architecture}
While the current production system operates reliably on local SQLite, Qdrant, and Ollama, we propose an
enterprise scaling roadmap for provincial-scale deployments:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Semantic Vector Caching (GPTCache)} \cite{gptcache}: A Redis vector cache before the router
to serve high-frequency queries in sub-15ms at cosine similarities $\ge 0.92$.
\item \textbf{Dynamic Fast/Slow Routing (RouteLLM)} \cite{routellm}: Routing straightforward procedural
queries to lightweight SLMs while reserving larger models for multi-hop statutory analysis.
\item \textbf{Continuous Memory Serving (vLLM)} \cite{vllm}: PagedAttention to eliminate KV-cache
fragmentation during high-concurrency civic service hours.
\end{itemize}

\section{Discussion \& Scientific Audit of Evaluation Methodology}

\subsection{The Empirical Fallacy of LLM-as-a-Judge for Statutory RAG}
Recent RAG literature relies heavily on synthetic LLM-as-a-judge frameworks such as RAGAS \cite{ragas}.
While the original RAGAS study demonstrated high human correlation on general-knowledge Wikipedia
queries (WikiEval), its validity when deployed with lightweight on-premise models in public
administration has remained unverified. We conducted an empirical calibration study
(\texttt{eval/ragas\_calib.py}) using local SLMs (Qwen3-4B and Qwen2.5-7B) to evaluate 92 administrative
QA pairs against negative controls:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item \textbf{Planted Hallucinations:} When an explicit fabrication (\emph{``This procedure is completely
free and completed within 1 hour at any municipal agency''}) was injected into legitimate answers,
Qwen2.5-7B identified the hallucination in only 51\% of cases (47/92), while Qwen3-4B caught only 30\%
(9/30).
\item \textbf{Inter-Judge Agreement:} Against an authoritative model baseline across 30 identical cases,
Qwen2.5-7B achieved inter-annotator agreement of \kpa{}\,$=0.02$ on Faithfulness and \kpa{}\,$=0.20$ on
Relevancy, falling drastically short of acceptable scientific reliability (\kpa{}\,$\ge 0.60$).
\end{enumerate}
These findings demonstrate that \textbf{relying uncritically on LLM-as-a-judge scores creates a false
sense of factual safety}. Consequently, \sys{} establishes a strict architectural paradigm:
administrative factuality is enforced via deterministic database anchoring and rule-based verification,
rather than generative post-hoc grading.

\subsection{Analysis of the Generalization Gap in Public Administration}
A comparison of the controlled developmental suite (DEV, 98\% Top-1) and the unseen blind suite (553
cases, 73\% Top-1) highlights the primary challenges of real-world deployment:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Lexical Divergence:} Colloquial queries frequently employ dialectal verbs (\emph{``xin giấy
chứng sinh''}, \emph{``nhập khẩu cho vợ''}) with low lexical overlap with codified procedure titles
(\emph{``Đăng ký khai sinh''}, \emph{``Đăng ký thường trú''}).
\item \textbf{Brother Procedure Ambiguity:} In civil status registration, closely related procedures
(e.g., \emph{Đăng ký kết hôn có yếu tố nước ngoài} vs.\ \emph{Đăng ký kết hôn trong nước}) share over
80\% of vocabulary. Without explicit clarification dialogue, single-turn search defaults to the domestic
procedure, depressing Top-1 while maintaining high Top-3 recall (80\%--87\%).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Known System \& Data Limitations}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item \textbf{Clarification Recall Bottleneck:} In the blind suite, only 47\% of ambiguous inquiries
successfully triggered clarification cards, with the remainder resolving to an assumed top candidate.
\item \textbf{National Portal Snapshot Omissions:} Standardized fees are published for only 467 of 1,350
procedures (34.6\%). For the remaining 883 procedures, the portal records no explicit fee schedule;
\sys{} strictly outputs a refusal (\emph{``Phí chưa được công bố chính thức''}) to avoid hallucination.
\item \textbf{Database String Parsing Anomalies:} Database audits revealed approximately 31 records where
currency punctuation was truncated during scraper ingestion (e.g., \texttt{"20.000"} parsed as 20 VND
instead of 20,000 VND). The strict engine implements regex-level currency sanitization to prevent
misleading cost outputs.
\item \textbf{Data Provenance Verification:} To quantify the fidelity of the scraped
gazetteer against the authoritative National Public Service Portal, we conducted a manual
provenance audit over a random sample of 50 procedure records, cross-checking fees,
processing times, legal basis, and decision numbers against the live portal pages. The
audit yielded a source-faithful rate of 84.4\% (211/250 fields; 95\% CI: 79\%--88\%),
rising to 93.5\% (187/200; CI: 89\%--96\%) when restricted to the four core
numerical/legal fields (fees, processing time, legal basis, decision number).
\end{enumerate}

% ======================= 6. CONCLUSION =======================
\section{Conclusion \& Future Work}
This paper presented \sys{}, an enterprise context-aware legal artificial intelligence assistant designed
for Vietnamese public administration. By integrating a deterministic \textbf{Strict Engine (System 3)}
with an extensible \textbf{Friendly Engine (System 4)} and enforcing multi-turn \emph{Clarify State
Isolation}, the architecture resolves the twin hazards of hallucination and conversational drift.
Extensive empirical evaluations across 1,352 standardized test cases demonstrate:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{78\% behavioral accuracy and 73\% Top-1 retrieval} (MRR 0.759, nDCG@5 0.752) on the official
553-case blind evaluation suite.
\item \textbf{85\% behavioral accuracy and 78\% Top-1} (MRR 0.824, nDCG@5 0.805) on the official
HOLDOUT-4 benchmark.
\item \textbf{91\% general accuracy (29/32), 100\% document QA citation accuracy, and zero web numerical
hallucinations} on System 4.
\item \textbf{100\% PII redaction recall} on citizen identity numbers and telephone formats.
\item \textbf{100\% adversarial prompt injection resistance} across 11 distinct attack vectors.
\item Sub-100 ms pure algorithmic retrieval latency on commodity CPU hardware.
\end{itemize}
Future work will focus on expanding text-to-SQL aggregation for administrative procedure listings,
integrating regional dialectal query expansion, and implementing semantic vector caching (GPTCache) and
continuous memory serving (vLLM) for provincial-scale deployment.
